RAIKU FIELD BUILD · TIMELOCK

A scheduler for blockspace finds its own blind spot.

Raiku's own materials name the requirement directly: multi-leg institutional trades that must execute whole, because partial execution leaves an exposure that was never part of the mandate. Raiku also documents exactly one way to make several transactions land as a single unit, same block, JIT, bundled. Everything else, a sell now and a hedge two slots later, gets each leg a real guarantee individually and nothing that spans between them. Timelock takes a declared multi-step workflow, assigns every leg to a real Raiku primitive, and then computes the one number nobody has published: how many slots of unguaranteed exposure survive even when every leg is scheduled correctly.

02 · Compiled plan

Every leg, classified against a real rule

A step with a known future window and no live condition gets AOT, a reserved slot at a predictable price. A step that reacts to a live condition gets JIT, a sealed-bid auction at market fee plus 5 percent minimum. Two adjacent steps marked atomic must be submitted as one JIT bid before either outcome is known, that's the real trade, a guarantee in exchange for the ability to react.

04 · The receipt, not a status light

A chained, checkable record of the plan

Raiku's real pre-confirmation is a cryptographic guarantee, not a green checkmark. This chains one real SHA-256 digest per step, each folding in the previous step's hash, so changing anything upstream visibly changes every hash after it, computed live in your browser via the Web Crypto API, nothing simulated about the hashing itself.

05 · What Raiku actually guarantees, checked against the source

The real primitives, and the real ceiling

AOT reservationDocumented. Select a future slot or window, English auction, predictable pricing. Used here for any step with a known window and no live condition.
JIT auctionDocumented. Sealed-bid, immediate, minimum bid is the live priority fee plus 5 percent. Used here for any step with a condition that can only be evaluated live.
Same-block atomic groupDocumented, JIT only: "a simultaneous purchase and sale is only valid if both legs execute together, is treated atomically." That's a single block. Used here when you mark two adjacent steps atomic.
Pre-confirmationDocumented: a cryptographic guarantee issued ahead of the slot stating a transaction will execute in slot N. This is the real receipt, not an invented status light.
Cross-slot atomicity for ordinary workflowsNot documented. Nothing published guarantees that a step two or more slots later either lands or the earlier step unwinds. This is the gap this page measures.
Full workflow atomicity, if you need it todayRequires standing up an Extension, your own off-chain execution environment, settling back to Solana via a cryptographic proof, chunked through Proof Streaming if large. Real, but a different, much heavier build than declaring a workflow.

07 · What's real and what's assumed

Methodology

Documented and used as-is: the AOT and JIT mechanics above, the same-block atomic grouping rule, and pre-confirmation, all quoted from Raiku's own blog. The requirement itself, that multi-leg institutional trades must execute whole or not at all, is Raiku's own stated language from the Engineering Contract series, not an assumption. Solana's real slot time, 400 milliseconds, is used to convert the slot count into seconds.

Modeled and disclosed: the one-slot floor per non-atomic dependency is structural, two separate transactions cannot occupy the same instant unless explicitly bundled, so it holds regardless of network conditions. The contention slider above it is a disclosed assumption, an illustrative estimate of how many extra slots a JIT leg might need to win its auction under load, not a measured figure. Raiku's actual technical documentation and SDK remain gated behind their builder program pending public testnet, so nothing here submits to or reads from Raiku's real systems, the compiler's output is the plan a real integration would need to produce.
