Methodology & sources

Documented and used as-is: the AOT and JIT mechanics above, same-block atomic grouping, and pre-confirmation, quoted from Raiku's own blog. The "multi-leg trades must execute whole" requirement is Raiku's own language from the Engineering Contract series. Solana's real 400ms slot time converts slots to seconds.

Modeled and disclosed: the one-slot floor per non-atomic dependency is structural, two transactions can't land in the same instant unless bundled. The contention slider is a disclosed, illustrative assumption, not a measured figure. Raiku's SDK stays gated pending public testnet, so nothing here reads from Raiku's real systems.
